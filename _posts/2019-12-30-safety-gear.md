# Soldering

<div class="encart red" markdown="1">
The fumes from leaded solder don't themselves contain lead. The iron isn't hot enough to vaporize the metal itself. - [Resources to get good at soldering?](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49586049)
- The fumes are from the flux, and breathing those can be problematic. 
- The immediate health risk in using leaded solder is all about touch and ingestion. Handling the solder gets lead on your fingers... But then if you touch food or mucous membranes (pick your nose, put your finger in your mouth, rub your eyes, etc.), you can get lead in your system. Not much, but once there, it doesn't leave, so accumulates over time.
