## Software Factory
<div class="encart warning" markdown="1">
The problem with the software factory is not that the technology is bad.
It's that we're applying a 19th century industrial mental model to a 21st century cognitive shift.
</div>

- [ Senior Engineers Are Using AI Wrong and Software Factories Are Dumb ](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4Oq_gdhjYUw)
  - how to organise code in the era of Ai?
  - how to reimagine the work, and not stay attached to standard factory?
    - [The Agentic Software Factory is the Wrong Metaphor](https://robwest.info/articles/the-agentic-software-factory-is-the-wrong-metaphor/)
      - A factory assumees that design is solved
        - but figuring out what to build and how it should behave is the work: a ticket is not a blueprint
      - The process of writing software changes our understanding of the specification while we are building it.
        - it's not spec in and software out
        - Design engineering evolve a specification as we learn more about the problem.
      - An assembly line is meant to eliminate variations.
        - Design engineering benefits from it.
        - This is how we compare, test, and find better approaches.
      - You have to feed a Factory
        - That's create pressure to formalize requirements before we understand the problem
          - Ambiguity doesn't just disappear.
          - The agent will fill the gap with best guesses and very confidenttly produce the wront thing.
