> Nemo support .desktop file that when opened open embedded url - [ChatGPT](https://chatgpt.com/share/6a759f40-cda0-83eb-ba9f-2c02e7c08b9f)

```ini
[Desktop Entry]
Version=1.0
Type=Link
Name=ChatGPT
URL=https://chat.openai.com/
Icon=web-browser
```
